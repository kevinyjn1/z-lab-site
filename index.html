Matter in extremes,
machine for extremes

The Z Lab · University of Michigan · 2006 to today

Abstract

We combine statistical physics, molecular simulations, artificial intelligence, and neutron scattering experiments to understand rare events and long timescale phenomena in complex materials: liquids, glasses, and complex fluids at interfaces, under extreme conditions, or driven away from equilibrium. We also build robots for extreme environments: swarm robots, wheel-leg hybrid robots, and soft robots.

From fundamental to applied, both pillars serve one mission: a sustainable, resilient, and secure energy infrastructure.
