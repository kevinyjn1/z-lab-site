What we do

Our research in two words: Matter and Machine.

In the Matter domain, we synergistically integrate statistical physics, molecular simulations, artificial intelligence, and neutron scattering experiments to extend our understanding of rare/extreme events and long timescale phenomena in complex materials.

Concurrently, on the Machine front, we build robots for extreme environments, including swarm robots, wheel-leg hybrid robots, and soft robots.

Domain 01

Matter

Liquids, glasses, and complex fluids at interfaces, under extreme conditions, or driven away from equilibrium.

  • Physics and chemistry of liquids, glasses, and complex fluids
  • Statistical mechanics, accelerated molecular simulations, and understandable AI
  • Neutron scattering, sources, and instrumentation
  • Rare/extreme events and long timescale phenomena
