4Software

Tools we build for simulating and analyzing liquids, soft materials, and robots.

Z-Laboratory on GitHub

  • LiquidLib

    C++

    A C++ library for computing statistical quantities of liquids and liquid-like matter from atomic trajectories, with applications to neutron scattering experiments.

    PaperTwo of its functions, live

  • Kraken

    MOOSE

    A MOOSE-based multiphysics simulation platform for soft materials and robots.

    DocumentationPapersWhat it models

  • Ascent Dynamics

    Sampling

    Ascent Dynamics (AD), for efficient rare event and transition pathway sampling and global optimization.

  • All-atom metadynamics

    Sampling

    A variation of the metadynamics method for direct potential energy landscape sampling.

    See it at work

  • REMA Toolkit

    MATLAB

    A MATLAB toolbox for analyzing relaxation-excitation modes and the statistics of energy landscape in liquids and complex materials.

  • ST2 Water Model

    GROMACS

    Implementation of the ST2 water potential in GROMACS.
